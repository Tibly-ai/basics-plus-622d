What we do

Complete locksmith solutions, cut and installed by hand

Standard house keys, high-security keys, custom-cut specialty keys: we make them all in the shop while you wait. We also install everything from basic deadbolts to full security systems for apartments, brownstones, offices and storefronts.

01

24/7 emergency lockout service

Apartment door, office door, storefront gate. Call any hour and a licensed locksmith is dispatched to get you back inside without wrecking the door.

02

Key cutting and duplication

House keys, mailbox keys, restricted and high-security keys. Cut on the spot at the counter and tested before you leave.

03

Lock installation and rekeying

New deadbolts, cylinder swaps, and rekeying for homes and businesses. One key for every door, or separate keys per tenant.

04

Safe and vault services

We handle all safe types, residential through commercial vaults, including expert installation, servicing and combination changes.

05

Security system consultation

Walk the building with us and we will plan the hardware: access control, master key systems, reinforced strikes, and the install that follows.
