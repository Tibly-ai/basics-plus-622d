What a Mobile Visit Covers

24/7 emergency lockouts. Apartment doors, building front doors, offices, and rolling storefront gates. Tell us the cross street, the type of door, whether the key snapped off inside, and whether you have ID or a super/managing agent who can vouch for you. A licensed locksmith is dispatched and picks locking cylinders non-destructively first — drilling is the last resort, and you're told before it happens.

Rekey on the spot. If your keys are lost, stolen, or you just don't know who else has a copy, the locksmith can swap the pins in the existing cylinder and cut you a fresh set right there, so every old key stops working immediately.

Lock installation, on location. From a basic deadbolt to a high-security cylinder, our mobile locksmiths install and replace hardware for apartments, brownstones, offices, and storefronts without you having to bring the door to us.

High-security and restricted keys. Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Abloy, Schlage Primus, and other restricted keyways are cut and tested against your cylinder. Bring your key control card or authorization if your building's system requires one.

Safe and vault work. We handle all safe types, from a residential floor safe to a commercial vault, including installation and combination changes — arranged as an on-site visit when the unit can't come to the counter.

Security system consultation. A locksmith walks the space with you and tells you what actually needs upgrading — access control, master key systems, reinforced strikes — before any install is scheduled.

FAQ

Do you offer a mobile locksmith NYC service, or do I have to come to a shop? Both. Call (347) 549-9077 and we dispatch a licensed locksmith to your door from whichever of our three shops is closest — Union Square, Upper West Side, or Midtown East. You can also walk into any of the three storefronts with a key in hand.

How fast can a mobile locksmith get to me in NYC? It depends on the hour and crosstown traffic, so we give you a real arrival window on the phone rather than a number we can't keep. Downtown calls run from Union Square, uptown from the Upper West Side shop, and Midtown from 845 2nd Avenue.

Will the mobile locksmith destroy my lock to get in? Most apartment and office lockouts open through picking and non-destructive entry, which is always our first approach. If a cylinder has to be drilled, we tell you before touching it and quote the replacement at the same time. Because the locksmith carries shop stock, the new cylinder usually goes in during the same visit.

Can the mobile locksmith rekey my apartment on the spot? Yes. If your keys are gone for good, we can rekey the cylinder during the same visit and cut you a fresh set, so the old keys stop working immediately.

What areas does your mobile locksmith service cover? All of Manhattan — Union Square, the East Village, Greenwich Village, the West Village, Flatiron, Gramercy, NoHo, SoHo, the Lower East Side, Chelsea, the Upper West Side, Lincoln Square, Manhattan Valley, Morningside Heights, Columbus Circle, and Midtown.

What should I have ready when I call for a mobile locksmith? Your cross street and apartment/suite number, the type of door (apartment, building front, office, or rolling gate), whether the key broke off inside the cylinder, and ID or someone on-site (super, building manager, lease) who can confirm you belong there.
