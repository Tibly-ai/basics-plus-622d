What to Do the Moment You're Locked Out

Stay by your door. We cannot open an apartment without the resident present, so if you've stepped away, come back before the locksmith arrives. While you wait, have these four things ready for the phone call:

  1. Your cross street — the address plus the nearest corner, and your apartment number. In a city of short blocks, the corner gets our locksmith to the right door faster.
  2. The type of door — apartment door, building front door, office suite, or a rolling storefront gate. Tell us if it's metal or wood, one lock or two.
  3. Whether the key broke off inside the cylinder — a snapped key is a different job than a locked door with keys left upstairs. If it broke off, stop turning it and leave the piece where it is.
  4. ID showing the address — a driver's license, lease, a piece of mail, or a super or building manager who can vouch for you. We open doors for the people who belong behind them, so having this ready speeds things up.

What Happens After You Call

A licensed locksmith is dispatched from the nearest shop. Downtown calls run out of our Union Square shop, uptown calls out of the Upper West Side shop, and Midtown calls out of our 845 2nd Avenue location. We tell you on the phone who is coming and give you a real arrival window based on the hour and crosstown traffic — not a number we can't keep.

You hear the price before anything starts. The locksmith looks at the door, explains what the job takes, and quotes it on the spot. Nothing gets picked, drilled, or opened until you agree to the number.

The door opens without wrecking the frame. Non-destructive entry and picking come first, always. Most apartment lockouts open this way. If a cylinder genuinely has to be drilled, we tell you before we touch it and quote the replacement lock at the same time.

We rekey on the spot if your keys are gone for good. Lost in a cab, taken with a stolen bag, or simply missing — if the old keys are a security risk, we can rekey the cylinder right there and cut you a fresh set, so the old keys stop working immediately.
