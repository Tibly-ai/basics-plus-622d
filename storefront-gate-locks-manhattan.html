How a commercial job runs

Same four steps whether it is one padlock or a master key chart for a twelve-suite floor.

  1. 01

    You call and describe the door

    Gate, glass storefront, wood office door, what the lock looks like, what it is doing. Most of the time we can tell you on the phone whether it is a same-visit fix.

  2. 02

    We look, then we quote

    A locksmith inspects the hardware on site and gives you the price before anything is drilled, pulled, or ordered. If you want it in writing for building management or an owner, we write it.

  3. 03

    The work gets done

    Cylinders swapped, gates relocked, exit devices adjusted, keys cut on the spot. For master systems we chart the whole floor first so future changes stay simple.

  4. 04

    You get the key record

    Every key we cut for your account is logged with the door it opens. When a manager changes or a set goes missing, one call tells us exactly what needs rekeying.

Questions managers ask

If yours is not here, call the shop. We would rather talk it through than have you guess.

How fast can you get to my gate?
We answer the phone around the clock and dispatch from whichever of our three Manhattan shops is closest to you. Timing depends on the hour, the traffic, and what else is running, so we give you a realistic window when you call rather than a promise we cannot keep.
Can you replace a gate lock on the same visit?
Usually yes. We stock common gate padlocks, hasps, and cylinder gate locks on the truck. If your gate uses an unusual locking point or a part that has to be ordered, we secure the gate that visit and schedule the permanent replacement.
Do you set up master key systems for offices?
Yes. We chart multi-tenant floors and suites so a super or office manager carries one key that opens everything, while each tenant key only opens their own door. The chart stays on file with us, which makes adding a door or replacing a lost master straightforward later.
Can you rekey after an employee leaves without replacing the locks?
That is the normal fix. We repin the existing cylinders to a new key, cut a fresh set for current staff, and the old keys stop working. The hardware stays, so it costs less and takes less time than a full replacement. We only recommend new locks when the existing ones are worn out or damaged.
Do you work with building management?
Regularly. We coordinate with supers and management offices on shared entrances, stair doors, and anything covered by the building's own keyway, and we will send a written quote to management before the work starts if that is what your lease requires.
Who can order copies of our keys?
Only people you authorize on the account. Every key we cut is logged against your doors, so a manager can see who holds what. High-security restricted keyways add another layer, since those blanks are not available anywhere else.
