Locksmith NYC Services

Everything below is done by our own licensed staff, on the spot, at the counter or at your door.

24/7 emergency lockout service Locked out at 6am or 2am, we pick up the phone. A licensed locksmith is dispatched to get you back inside without wrecking the lock. Picking and non-destructive entry come first; drilling is a last resort, and you're told the price before anything is touched.

Key cutting and duplication Standard house keys, building keys, high-security keys (Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Abloy, Schlage Primus, and other restricted keyways), and custom-cut specialty blanks — cut on calibrated machines and tested in a matching cylinder before you leave.

Lock installation and rekeying Basic deadbolts through advanced security hardware, for apartments, brownstones, offices, and storefronts. New tenant, lost key, or a change of hands — we rekey so the old keys stop working, or install new hardware when the cylinder is worn, damaged, or forced.

Safe and vault services All safe types, from a residential floor safe to a commercial vault, including installation and combination changes.

Security system consultation We walk the space with you and tell you what actually needs upgrading — access control, master key systems, reinforced strikes — then handle the install.

What Happens When You Call

  1. You tell us the cross street, the type of door, and whether the key is broken off inside.
  2. A licensed locksmith is dispatched from whichever of our three shops is closest to you.
  3. The locksmith looks at the lock, explains the job, and quotes it before touching anything.
  4. The door opens without wrecking the frame wherever possible. If keys are gone for good, we can rekey the cylinder and cut a fresh set on the same visit.
