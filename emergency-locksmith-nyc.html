What Happens When You Call

A licensed locksmith is dispatched from our own storefront, not a subcontractor pulled off an app. We tell you on the phone who is coming. You hear the price before any work starts — the locksmith looks at the door, explains the job, and quotes it on the spot. Nothing gets drilled, picked, or opened until you agree.

The door opens without wrecking the frame. Picking and non-destructive entry come first, and most apartment and office lockouts open with the cylinder and frame untouched. Drilling is a last resort on a seized or high-security cylinder, and we tell you before it happens.

If the keys are gone for good — lost in a cab, taken with a stolen bag — we can rekey the cylinder and cut a fresh set while we're there, so the old keys stop working.

What Emergency Lockout Service NYC Really Means at Basics Plus

Not every lockout is the same job, and we treat them differently:

  • Apartment lockouts — door pulled shut behind you, keys inside. We cannot open an apartment without the resident present, so stay by the door if you can.
  • Building front door lockouts — locked out of the vestibule or lobby entrance, not just your unit.
  • Office suite lockouts — commercial doors, often with a landlord or building manager to loop in.
  • Storefront gate lockouts — rolling metal gates at closing time or first thing in the morning.
  • Broken key extraction — a key snapped off inside the cylinder is a different job than a locked door with keys upstairs. If this happens to you, stop turning it and leave the piece where it is.

What to Tell Us on the Call

The faster we know what we're walking into, the faster the right tools come out with the locksmith:

  • Your cross street — address plus the nearest corner, and apartment or suite number.
  • The type of door — apartment door, building front door, office suite, or a rolling storefront gate; metal or wood, one lock or two.
