Tell us four things when you call

The faster we know what we are walking into, the faster the right tools come with the locksmith. Thirty seconds on the phone saves a second trip.

01

Your cross street

Broadway and 92nd reads very differently than Riverside and 110th. Give us the corner and the building number.

02

The type of door

Apartment door, building front door, office, or a rolling gate on the avenue. Pre-war mortise locks need different tools than a modern cylinder.

03

Doorman or walk-up

In a doorman or co-op building, tell us who we check in with. In a walk-up, tell us the floor and whether the vestibule door is locked too.

04

Did the key break off

A snapped key inside the cylinder is an extraction job. Say so on the phone and the extraction kit and a blank come with us.

Uptown lockouts we handle

Old buildings, heavy doors, and hardware that has been in the frame since before the tenant was born. We know these locks.

Apartment and co-op lockouts

Door shut behind you, keys on the kitchen counter. We open it and, where the board or managing agent requires it, work with building staff on the paperwork side.

Pre-war mortise locks

Common all over the West Side. Old mortise bodies are worn and stubborn. We open them carefully and can replace the cylinder or the whole body if it is past saving.

Building front doors

Vestibule and lobby doors, panic hardware, and intercom-release strikes for supers and managing agents who need a door back in service tonight.

Storefront and gate locks

Rolling gates and shop doors on Broadway, Amsterdam and Columbus. A gate that will not open at 6am costs you the whole day, so we treat it as an emergency.

Broken key extraction

Key snapped in the cylinder. We pull the broken half, check whether the cylinder survived, and cut you a fresh key from the remaining piece or the keyway.

Rekeying after lost keys

Keys stolen or missing with your address attached. We rekey the cylinder while we are there and hand you a new set before we leave.

Questions people ask from the hallway

Still standing there. Call and ask anything else: 212-510-8320.

How fast can you get here? +

It depends on traffic, the time of night, and how many jobs are already out. When you call we tell you honestly what the run uptown looks like right then, instead of promising a number we cannot keep.

Do I need ID to have my door opened? +

Yes. We need to see that you belong on the other side of that door: ID with the address, a lease, a piece of mail, or confirmation from the doorman or super. If your ID is locked inside, tell us on the phone and we will sort out what works.

Will the door or the lock be damaged? +

Our first move is always non-destructive entry, which leaves the frame and the door alone. Some seized or high-security cylinders have to be drilled and replaced. If that is the case, the locksmith explains it and quotes the replacement before touching the lock.

Can you rekey the lock right away? +

In most cases yes. If your keys are lost or stolen we rekey the cylinder on the visit and cut new keys so the old ones stop working. For a whole apartment or a building line, see apartment rekeying.

Do you work in doorman and co-op buildings? +

Constantly. We are licensed and insured, we check in at the desk, and we can provide a certificate of insurance when the managing agent asks for one. Tell us the building rules when you call so nothing holds us up in the lobby.
