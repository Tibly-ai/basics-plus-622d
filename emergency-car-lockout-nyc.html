How Basics Plus Handles an Emergency Call

Whatever the door, the process is the same:

  1. A licensed locksmith is dispatched from our own storefront — Union Square, On Broadway, or Midtown, whichever is closest — not a subcontractor pulled off an app.
  2. You hear the price before any work starts. The locksmith looks at the lock, explains the job, and quotes it on the spot.
  3. Non-destructive entry comes first. Picking and non-destructive methods are tried before anything else; drilling is a last resort on a seized or high-security cylinder, and you're told before it happens.
  4. Rekeying on the spot if keys are gone for good. If keys were lost or stolen, we can rekey the cylinder and cut a fresh set while we're there, so the old keys stop working.

FAQ

Does Basics Plus handle emergency car lockouts in NYC? Our published emergency lockout coverage is built around property doors — apartments, building front doors, office suites, and storefront gates — plus broken key extraction. If your lockout is a vehicle, call (347) 549-9077 and ask directly; the shop will tell you on the phone whether it's a fit before dispatching anyone.

What areas does Basics Plus's emergency lockout service cover? All of Manhattan, dispatched from three storefronts: Union Square (covering Union Square, Greenwich Village, the East Village, West Village, Flatiron, Gramercy, NoHo, SoHo, the Lower East Side, and Chelsea), On Broadway at 83rd Street (covering the Upper West Side, Lincoln Square, Manhattan Valley, Morningside Heights, Columbus Circle, the Harlem edge, and across the park to the Upper East Side), and Midtown at 845 2nd Avenue (Midtown offices, storefronts, and apartment buildings).

How fast can someone get to me? Arrival time depends on the hour and crosstown traffic, so Basics Plus gives you a real arrival window on the phone rather than a number they can't keep. Downtown calls run out of Union Square, uptown calls out of the Upper West Side shop, and Midtown calls out of 845 2nd Avenue.

Will opening my door damage the lock? Most apartment and office lockouts open without drilling — that's always the first approach. If a cylinder has to be drilled, you're told before it's touched, and the replacement is quoted at the same time. Because the shop carries stock, the new cylinder usually goes in during the same visit.

What should I tell Basics Plus when I call about a lockout? Your cross street and apartment or suite number, what kind of door it is (apartment, building front door, office suite, or rolling gate), whether the key broke off inside the cylinder, and whether you have ID or paperwork showing the address.

What if my key snapped off inside the lock? Stop turning it and leave the piece where it is. Broken key extraction is handled as its own job, separate from a standard lockout, and the locksmith brings the right tools if you flag it on the call.
