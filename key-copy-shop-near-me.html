What We Cut at the Counter

Bring in whatever key you have and we'll tell you on the spot what it takes:

Standard house and building keys. The most common walk-in request. We cut on the spot and test the copy in the lock before you leave the counter.

High-security and restricted keys. Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Abloy, and Schlage Primus are patented keyways that hardware store kiosks physically cannot cut — they don't stock the blanks, and the machines aren't built for the angled cuts or side milling these systems require. We stock the blanks and cut them on calibrated machines at all three shops. Restricted systems may require a key control card or building authorization; call ahead and we'll tell you exactly what to bring.

Custom-cut specialty keys. Mailbox keys, cabinet keys, and less common blanks — cut in the shop while you wait when the job allows.

Every key, standard or high-security, gets checked in a matching cylinder before you walk out. That's how we catch a bad cut before it becomes a jammed lock at your door.

Why People Come to a Storefront Instead of a Kiosk

A self-serve kiosk can't check authorization records, can't reach the milled surfaces on a high-security blank, and won't test the result. At Basics Plus, you talk to the person actually cutting your key. If the blank you need isn't one we stock, we'll say so on the phone before you make the trip.
