What a Local Locksmith Handles at Basics Plus

The work covers the full range a Manhattan resident or business is likely to need, all done in-house:

  • 24/7 emergency lockout service — a licensed locksmith gets to your door day or night and gets you back inside without wrecking the lock. Non-destructive entry (picking, not drilling) comes first; drilling is a last resort on a seized or high-security cylinder.
  • Key cutting and duplication — standard house keys, building keys, high-security keys, and custom-cut specialty keys, cut on the spot and checked in the lock before you leave.
  • Lock installation and rekeying — basic deadbolts through advanced security hardware, for homes and businesses; rekeying resets a door so old keys stop working.
  • Safe and vault services — all safe types, from a residential floor safe to a commercial vault, including installation and combination changes.
  • Security system consultation — a locksmith walks the space, tells you what actually needs upgrading, then implements it.

How a Job Works, Start to Finish

  1. You describe the door, lock, or safe — by phone, through the contact form, or standing at the counter with a key in hand.
  2. A licensed locksmith assesses the job — in person for lockouts and installs, or at the counter for key cutting.
  3. You hear the price before anything starts.
  4. Non-destructive entry comes first on every lockout; drilling is only used as a last resort on a seized or high-security cylinder.

This is the same process whether it's a single apartment rekey or a commercial storefront gate.
