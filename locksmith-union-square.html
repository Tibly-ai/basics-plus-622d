Do I need to bring the original key?
An original key is the fastest way to get an exact duplicate, so bring it if you have it. If the key is lost, bring the lock or the cylinder and we can work from that instead. For a door we cannot remove, call and we will come cut or rekey on site.
Can you rekey a co-op apartment?
Yes. Rekeying a co-op or condo door is routine work for us. Some buildings run a restricted or building-wide key system and require management approval first, so check with your board or super before we start. Once that is settled, the rekey itself is quick.
How fast can you reach a lockout downtown?
Our emergency line is answered 24/7 for emergency inquiries. Union Square, the Village and Gramercy are all a short trip from this shop, so call and we will tell you honestly when a locksmith can be at your door.
Do you cut mailbox keys?
We do. Mailbox and lobby-box keys are cut at the counter along with house and office keys. Bring the key you have, or the mailbox lock itself if the box has been changed.
Do you work on storefront gates?
Yes. We service and install locks on roll-down gates for shops and restaurants around University Place and Broadway, along with the cylinders on the door behind them. Call the shop with your gate type and we will tell you what the job involves.
