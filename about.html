What we do behind the counter

Locks are all we do here. Decades of key cutting, rekeying, and safe work taught us the doors in this city: prewar apartment jambs, storefront gates, office suites with a dozen tenants on one master system. We quote the job before we start it.

01

24/7 emergency lockout service

Locked out at 6am or 2am, we pick up the phone. A licensed locksmith gets to your door and gets you back inside without wrecking the lock.

02

Key cutting and duplication

House keys, building keys, high-security keys, and custom-cut specialty keys. Cut on the spot in the shop and checked in the lock before you walk out.

03

Lock installation and rekeying

Basic deadbolts through advanced security hardware, for homes and businesses. New tenant, lost key, or a change of hands: we rekey so the old keys stop working.

04

Safe and vault services

We handle all safe types, from a residential floor safe to a commercial vault, including expert installation and combination changes.

05

Security system consultation

Walk the space with us and we will tell you what actually needs upgrading, then implement it. Straight answers, transparent quotes, no hidden charges.
